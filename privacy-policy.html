Privacy Policy for the "TimePro" Application

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Welearn (developed by Seif Malogo / Sefu Rashidi Malogo; "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the TimePro mobile application (package: com.timetable.timepro, the "App").

1. Information We Collect and Process

Category of Data What Is Collected or Stored Purpose
Timetable & Schedule Data (Local Storage) Class schedules, lesson times, subjects, alarm settings, and app preferences created by you. Stored locally on your device to deliver timetable reminders, alarms, and widgets. We do not upload your personal schedules to our servers.
App Update Check Data Basic network request metadata (such as IP address) when checking for app version updates. To notify you when a newer version of TimePro is available.
Advertising, Device & Diagnostic Data Advertising ID (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate/coarse location (derived from IP), device/OS information, ad interactions, and crash/diagnostic logs. To serve and measure advertisements via Google AdMob (and enabled ad partners), detect invalid traffic/fraud, and maintain app stability.

2. Google AdMob and Third-Party Advertising

TimePro is supported by advertisements provided through Google AdMob and its ad technology partners:

  • Data Used by AdMob: Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, ad impression/click events, and diagnostic data to display, personalize (where permitted), and measure ads, as well as prevent fraud. Learn more at Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Your Ad & Consent Choices: Where required by law, you will be presented with a consent choice before personalized ads are served. You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy / Google > Ads).

3. Device Permissions

TimePro may request system permissions strictly to perform core timetable functions, such as scheduling exact alarms/notifications for class periods or managing ringer/silent modes during scheduled lessons. These permissions are used solely on your device for user-configured schedules.

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only processed by trusted third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob for advertising and standard hosting infrastructure for update checks) or disclosed if required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

TimePro is a general utility application for students, teachers, and individuals. We do not knowingly collect personal registration data from children under 13. Where applicable, ad requests are configured to comply with child-directed and age-appropriate advertising requirements.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because your timetable data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. For any privacy questions or requests regarding data processed through our services, contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated Last Updated date.
