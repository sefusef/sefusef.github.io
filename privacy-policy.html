Privacy Policy for the "Wonderz Learn" Application

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Welearn (developed and led by Sefu Rashidi Malogo; "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Wonderz Learn mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The table below outlines the categories of information that may be collected through the App and why they are used.

Category of Data What May Be Collected Purpose
Account & Profile Data User ID, username, role/account details, and email address (when provided for account access or support). To provide access to notes, lessons, quizzes, and study progress, and to respond to support requests.
Learning & Usage Data Saved notes, quiz results, lessons or modules viewed, video progress, coding/editor sessions, and app usage duration. To save learning progress, organize educational features, and improve app performance and usability.
Chat, Code & AI Interaction Data Messages sent in chat or collaborative features, code snippets shared or executed, and prompts/responses with AI assistance features. To power learning tools, answer academic questions, and maintain communication features.
Advertising, Device & Diagnostic Data Advertising ID (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate/coarse location (e.g., region/country derived from IP or location settings), device/OS details, ad impressions/interactions, and crash/diagnostic logs. To serve and measure advertisements via Google AdMob (and any enabled ad partners), prevent fraud/abuse, and maintain app stability.

2. Google AdMob, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Wonderz Learn integrates third-party services that may collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy terms:

  • Google AdMob & Advertising Partners: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements and support free access to educational content. Google AdMob and its ad technology partners may collect device identifiers (including the mobile Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, and to detect invalid traffic or fraud. You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services at Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Consent & Ad Personalization Choices: Depending on your region, you may see a consent prompt before personalized ads are shown (such as via Google's User Messaging Platform / a certified Consent Management Platform where required by applicable law). You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy / Google > Ads) or manage consent choices where available in the App.
  • Embedded YouTube Content: Where educational videos are embedded or linked from YouTube, viewing or interacting with those videos may transmit data (such as IP address, device info, and viewing activity) to YouTube/Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud, Backend & AI Services: Remote configuration, update checks, and AI/learning features may rely on cloud infrastructure or AI APIs to process requests and deliver content.

3. AI Assistant, Coding Tools, and Chat Features

  • AI & Study Assistance: Prompts and inputs submitted to AI features are processed to generate responses. Avoid entering sensitive personal data, passwords, financial information, or confidential records into AI prompts or code editors.
  • Chat & Shared Content: Messages or code shared in multi-user or collaborative spaces may be visible to other participants in that space. Please use caution before sharing personal details.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information as a traditional data broker. Information may be shared only in these cases:

  • Service Providers & Ad Networks: With trusted infrastructure, analytics, and advertising partners (including Google AdMob) that help operate, maintain, and monetize the App.
  • Legal & Safety Requirements: When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Welearn, users, or the public.
  • With Your Direction or Consent: When you choose to share content or authorize a specific feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Wonderz Learn is designed as a general educational application and is not directed to children under the age of 13 without appropriate parent, guardian, or school authorization where required by law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 in violation of applicable laws (such as COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without proper consent, please contact us using the details below so we can review and remove the information as appropriate. Ad requests are configured in accordance with applicable child-directed and age-of-consent settings where required.

6. Data Retention, Security, and Your Rights

  • Retention & Security: We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the App's features, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes, using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.
  • Your Rights & Requests: Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the App, or to object to/restrict certain processing. Contact us to submit a request.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the App, AdMob/SDK requirements, or applicable laws. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
